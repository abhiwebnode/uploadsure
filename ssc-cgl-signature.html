Frequently asked questions

Do I need to upload a photo for SSC CGL?

No. SSC CGL captures your photo live through webcam or phone camera during the application. You only need to upload the scanned signature.

What is the SSC CGL signature size?

Signature: 10–20 KB, JPG format, no pixel size set (we output 472×157). Notice: ~6.0 x 2.0 cm, black ink, no CAPITALS. Some sites say 4.0x2.0 cm / 140x60 px; KB range is the hard check.

Why does my SSC CGL upload fail even after resizing?

Portals check both a minimum and a maximum file size. A file that is too small is rejected just like one that is too big. This tool hits both ends of the range. Also make sure the file is a real JPG, not a renamed PNG.

Is my photo uploaded to your server?

No. Everything runs inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.
