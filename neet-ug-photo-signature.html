How to resize your NEET UG photo & signature

  1. Take a clear photo. For the photograph, stand against a plain white wall in good light. For the signature, sign on plain white paper with a black pen and photograph it from above.
  2. Drop it in the box above. It is cropped to 413×531 / 800×1200 / 400×150 / 1200×800 px and compressed into the allowed KB range.
  3. Download and upload. The file is ready for the NEET UG form. No editing needed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the NEET UG photograph size?

Photograph: 10–200 KB, JPG format, no pixel size set (we output 413×531). Passport size, white bg, ~80% face incl. ears, taken after 1 Jan of exam year.

What is the NEET UG postcard photo (4×6 in) size?

Postcard photo (4×6 in): 10–200 KB, JPG format, no pixel size set (we output 800×1200). 4x6 inch, name + date printed below.

What is the NEET UG signature size?

Signature: 10–30 KB, JPG format, no pixel size set (we output 400×150). 2026 bulletin reportedly 10-100 KB; older/other sources 4-30 KB. 10-30 window passes both.

What is the NEET UG left thumb impression size?

Left thumb impression: 50–200 KB, JPG format, no pixel size set (we output 1200×800). Left & right hand fingers + thumb impressions. Sources say 10-200 or 50-200; target 100 passes both.

Why does my NEET UG upload fail even after resizing?

Portals check both a minimum and a maximum file size. A file that is too small is rejected just like one that is too big. This tool hits both ends of the range. Also make sure the file is a real JPG, not a renamed PNG.

Is my photo uploaded to your server?

No. Everything runs inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.
