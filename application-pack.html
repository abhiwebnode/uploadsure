Frequently Asked Questions

What is the photo size for SSC CGL 2024?

SSC CGL requires a photograph estimated at 200×230 pixels (3.5×4.5 cm at 200 DPI), between 20–50 KB, in JPG format with a plain white or light background. Select SSC in the Application Pack above and your photo is fixed automatically to this spec.

What is the photo and signature size for UPSC Civil Services?

UPSC Civil Services requires a photograph of 350×350 pixels, between 40–100 KB, in JPG format. The signature must be 350×150 pixels, between 10–40 KB, on white paper with black or blue ink. Select UPSC in the Application Pack to fix both together.

What is the photo size for NEET UG?

NTA NEET UG requires three images: a passport-size photograph (10–200 KB), a postcard-size photograph (50–300 KB), and a signature (4–30 KB), all in JPG format. Select NTA NEET UG in the Application Pack above to fix all three at once.

What is the photo size for IBPS PO and SBI Clerk?

IBPS / SBI portals require a photograph of 200×230 pixels, between 20–50 KB, in JPG format. The signature must be 140×60 pixels, between 10–20 KB. Select IBPS / SBI in the Application Pack above — both are fixed automatically.

Why is my photo being rejected even after resizing?

Common causes beyond pixel size: (1) Format — many portals only accept JPG, not PNG or WebP. Use the Convert tool. (2) File size — portals check both a minimum and maximum KB, not just maximum. (3) Background — some portals require a specific background colour. (4) Some exams use Live Capture (webcam) rather than file upload — in that case the portal sets dimensions itself. The Application Pack flags which specs are confirmed versus estimated.

Is it safe to upload my Aadhaar card or PAN card here?

Yes. UploadSure processes all files entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to any server. You can verify this yourself: open browser DevTools (F12), go to the Network tab, and upload a file. You will see zero outbound file requests. The file stays in your device's RAM and is discarded when you close the tab.
