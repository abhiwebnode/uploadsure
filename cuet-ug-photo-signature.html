How to resize your CUET UG photo & signature

  1. Take a clear photo. For the photograph, stand against a plain white wall in good light. For the signature, sign on plain white paper with a black pen and photograph it from above.
  2. Drop it in the box above. It is cropped to 413×531 / 400×150 px and compressed into the allowed KB range.
  3. Download and upload. The file is ready for the CUET UG form. No editing needed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the CUET UG photograph size?

Photograph: 10–200 KB, JPG format, no pixel size set (we output 413×531). White bg, 80% face incl. ears.

What is the CUET UG signature size?

Signature: 10–30 KB, JPG format, no pixel size set (we output 400×150). 2026 bulletin 10-50 KB; older 4-30. 10-30 passes both.

Why does my CUET UG upload fail even after resizing?

Portals check both a minimum and a maximum file size. A file that is too small is rejected just like one that is too big. This tool hits both ends of the range. Also make sure the file is a real JPG, not a renamed PNG.

Is my photo uploaded to your server?

No. Everything runs inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.
