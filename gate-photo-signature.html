How to resize your GATE 2027 photo & signature

  1. Take a clear photo. For the photograph, stand against a plain white wall in good light. For the signature, sign on plain white paper with a black pen and photograph it from above.
  2. Drop it in the box above. It is cropped to 480×640 / 540×160 px and compressed into the allowed KB range.
  3. Download and upload. The file is ready for the GATE 2027 form. No editing needed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GATE 2027 photograph size?

Photograph: 5–600 KB, JPG format, 200–530 px wide (we output 480×640). Aspect 0.66-0.89, face 60-70% of frame.

What is the GATE 2027 signature size?

Signature: 3–300 KB, JPG format, 250–580 px wide (we output 540×160). Signature fills 70-80% of image. Black/dark blue ink.

Why does my GATE 2027 upload fail even after resizing?

Portals check both a minimum and a maximum file size. A file that is too small is rejected just like one that is too big. This tool hits both ends of the range. Also make sure the file is a real JPG, not a renamed PNG.

Is my photo uploaded to your server?

No. Everything runs inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.
