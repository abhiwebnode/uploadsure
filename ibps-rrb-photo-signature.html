Frequently asked questions

What is the IBPS RRB photograph size?

Photograph: 20–50 KB, JPG format, 200×230 px (preferred).

What is the IBPS RRB signature size?

Signature: 10–20 KB, JPG format, 140×60 px (preferred). No CAPITALS.

What is the IBPS RRB left thumb impression size?

Left thumb impression: 20–50 KB, JPG format, 240×240 px (preferred). Left thumb, black/blue ink on white.

What is the IBPS RRB handwritten declaration size?

Handwritten declaration: 50–100 KB, JPG format, 800×400 px (preferred). Handwritten in English, no CAPITALS.

Why does my IBPS RRB upload fail even after resizing?

Portals check both a minimum and a maximum file size. A file that is too small is rejected just like one that is too big. This tool hits both ends of the range. Also make sure the file is a real JPG, not a renamed PNG.

Is my photo uploaded to your server?
